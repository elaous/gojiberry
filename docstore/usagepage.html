Store usage over time

Counts and sizes of the whole store, sampled once an hour. They name no document and no person. The last {{.Retention.Hours}} hours are kept by the hour and the last {{.Retention.Days}} days by the day, in memory only: a restart starts the series again, and a missing row means no sample was taken then. Each row is the last sample of its period. The same numbers as JSON: history.
